Built on Experience, Driven by Reliability

At Sterk, LLC, we believe great electrical work starts with trust, experience, and genuine care for our customers. With over 45 years in the industry, Joe Sterkenburg built a reputation for reliable service and quality craftsmanship across Landenberg, PA, and surrounding communities.

Our journey began with one simple goal: to provide dependable electrical solutions that homeowners can count on year after year. What started as a small operation has grown into a respected name for electrician services, thanks to our unwavering commitment to safety, honesty, and attention to detail.

We take pride in being a licensed and insured master electrician who shows up on time, delivers solid work, and treats every home as if it were our own. From electrical repairs to EV charger and generator installations, we bring the same dedication and precision to every project, big or small.

Our clients aren’t just customers, they’re neighbors. That’s why we go the extra mile to ensure every job is done right the first time, with results that last. At Sterk, LLC, reliability isn’t just our promise; it’s how we’ve done business for over four decades.
