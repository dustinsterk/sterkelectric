FAQs

1. How long does generator installation take?

Most installations take one to two days, depending on your system type and electrical setup. We handle the entire process efficiently, from preparation to final testing.

2. Do you install both standby and portable generators?

Yes. We offer standby generator installation for permanent backup and portable systems for smaller power needs or budget-friendly options.

3. What is a transfer switch, and why do I need one?

A transfer switch automatically connects your generator to your home’s electrical system during outages, providing seamless power without manual input.

4. Do I need a permit for generator installation?

Yes, but you don’t need to worry about it; we manage all permits and inspections to ensure full code compliance and safe operation.

5. How often should I maintain my generator?

We recommend annual maintenance and periodic load testing to ensure your generator runs efficiently and is ready when you need it most.
