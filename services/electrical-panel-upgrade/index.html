FAQs

1. How do I know if I need an electrical panel upgrade?

If your lights flicker, breakers trip often, or your panel feels warm, it’s likely time for an upgrade. Homes over 30 years old usually need modern electrical panels.

2. How long does it take to upgrade a panel?

Most electrical panel upgrade projects are completed in one day, including inspection and testing. Complex jobs with rewiring may take longer.

3. Can you repair my existing electrical panel?

Yes. We provide electrical panel repair for worn breakers, loose connections, or faulty wiring that can be safely restored without full replacement.

4. Is my home safe with an old fuse box?

Fuse boxes are outdated and often unsafe for today’s electrical loads. We offer fuse-to-breaker conversions to bring your system up to code and eliminate fire risks.

5. What’s included in a full panel replacement?

A main panel upgrade includes removing the old panel, installing a new breaker system, grounding, labeling circuits, and testing for optimal safety and performance.
