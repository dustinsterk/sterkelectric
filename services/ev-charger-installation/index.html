FAQs

1. How long does EV charger installation take?

Most residential installations take 2–4 hours, depending on your electrical panel and charger type. We complete all required testing and inspections the same day for your convenience.

2. Do I need to upgrade my electrical panel for an EV charger?

In some cases, yes. If your current system can’t support a 240V connection, we’ll recommend a safe panel upgrade to handle the added power load effectively.

3. Can you install Tesla Wall Connectors or other branded chargers?

Absolutely. We install Tesla, Rivian, BMW, Toyota, and other manufacturer-approved chargers, ensuring compatibility and proper setup for your vehicle.

4. Will you handle permits and code compliance?

Yes. We manage all permits and ensure your charger meets NEC codes and local regulations for safe, approved installation.

5. What type of EV charger is ideal for my home?

Most homeowners benefit from a Level 2 charger for faster charging and reliability. We’ll help you choose the right brand and setup for your home’s electrical system.
